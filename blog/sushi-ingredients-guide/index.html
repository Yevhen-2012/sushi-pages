Essential Ingredients for Making Sushi

Key Ingredients

To create authentic sushi, you need high-quality ingredients. The primary components include sushi rice, nori (seaweed), fresh fish, and various vegetables. Sushi rice is seasoned with rice vinegar, sugar, and salt, giving it a unique flavor that complements the fish.

Choosing Fresh Fish

When selecting fish, freshness is paramount. Look for fish that is bright in color and has a clean smell. Popular choices include tuna, salmon, and eel. Additionally, consider using vegetables like cucumber and avocado for added texture and flavor.
